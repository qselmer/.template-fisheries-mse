# How to use this template

> Spanish is the primary documentation language. [Versión en español](TEMPLATE_USAGE.md)

Create one repository per real species, stock, or fishery. Replace the example fields in `species_profile.yml`, define management objectives and risk tolerances, document the real management system and available evidence, populate the traceability registries, and keep unsupported choices as `TBD` until they are scientifically justified.

See [`README.md`](README.md) for the overall logic and [`REPOSITORY_MAP.md`](REPOSITORY_MAP.md) for folder-by-folder navigation.
