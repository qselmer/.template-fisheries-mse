# 02_stock_ecology

Document the biological and ecological processes that may affect management decisions: growth, maturity, natural mortality, fecundity, longevity, recruitment, stock structure, movement, distribution, aggregation, seasonality, and ecosystem interactions when relevant.

Complexity should be adapted to the species and management question. Processes such as episodic recruitment, regime shifts, spatial structure, or trophic roles should be included only when they could materially affect strategy performance.
